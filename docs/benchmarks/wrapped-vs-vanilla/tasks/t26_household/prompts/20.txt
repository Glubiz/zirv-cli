`python -m ledgerlite member rename OLD NEW --store PATH` renames a member
and prints `Renamed member OLD to NEW` (OLD in its stored spelling, NEW
with surrounding whitespace stripped).

- OLD is matched case-insensitively; unknown is the usual `error: unknown
  member: NAME`. NEW must follow the same rules as a new member's name: not
  empty (`error: member name cannot be empty`) and unique -- if it matches
  any OTHER member, archived ones included, it's `error: member already
  exists: NEW`. Renaming a member to a different capitalisation of their own
  name (`Ana` -> `ANA`) is fine, and OLD checks come before NEW checks.
- The new name has to show up everywhere the old one was stored: the member
  entry (archived status unchanged, same position in the list), every
  expense's payer, weights and shares (keeping their order), and the
  entries of every transfer. Afterwards every command shows exactly the
  same numbers as before, just under the new name -- balances, settle-up
  suggestions, reports, `expense show`, `account NEW`, and so on -- and
  `account OLD` is now an unknown member.
- It's a household change like the others, so it goes in the history
  (label `member rename OLD NEW`, OLD as stored, NEW as stripped) and can be
  undone. Remember what we decided about the history being append-only:
  entries that are already there are never rewritten, so they keep showing
  the old name.
