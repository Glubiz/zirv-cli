What I need next: a way to correct an expense that was entered wrong,
without deleting it and re-adding it.

`python -m ledgerlite expense edit ID [--amount AMOUNT] [--payer NAME]
[--desc TEXT] [--date YYYY-MM-DD] --store PATH`

- At least one of the four options is required, otherwise `error: nothing
  to edit`. An unknown id is `error: no such expense: ID` (same as `expense
  show`). The payer, amount and date are validated exactly like in `expense
  add`, same messages; if anything is invalid the expense is left entirely
  unchanged.
- Changing the amount re-splits the expense: same participants, same
  weights it was originally split with, and the leftover cents handled the
  way they are for any expense of that id. Changing only the payer,
  description or date leaves the shares alone.
- It prints `Updated expense ID` and `expense show ID` / `balance` / the
  rest immediately reflect the new numbers.
