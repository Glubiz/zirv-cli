Now the thing everyone actually wants to know: who is ahead and who owes?

Add `python -m ledgerlite balance --store PATH`. It prints one line per
member, in the order they were added: `NAME: AMOUNT`, where AMOUNT is what
the member has paid out for the household's expenses minus the sum of
their shares of all expenses, with exactly two decimals. So someone who has
paid more than their share has a positive balance (the household owes them
money), and someone who has paid less has a negative one, printed with a
leading minus sign, e.g. `Ben: -10.00`. A member with nothing going on is
`0.00`. With no members at all it prints `No members.`

Keep the calculation in `household.py` as its own function rather than
inside the CLI handler -- I have a feeling other commands are going to need
the same numbers.
