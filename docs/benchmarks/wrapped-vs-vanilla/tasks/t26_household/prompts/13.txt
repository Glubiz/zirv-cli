I got something wrong about transfers and I want to change it. Right now
every transfer moves the balances, but people also move money around for
reasons that have nothing to do with the shared expenses (Cara lending Ben
cash for a train ticket, say), and that shouldn't touch what the flat owes.
So from now on a transfer only counts toward balances when it is explicitly
a settling one.

- `transfer add` gets a new flag `--settles`. With it, the transfer behaves
  exactly like transfers do today (sender's balance up, receiver's down).
  WITHOUT it, the transfer is just recorded: it still gets stored as its
  pair of entries and shows up in `transfer list` and `account NAME`, but it
  has no effect at all on `balance`, `settle suggest`, or the `Sent`,
  `Received` and `Net` lines of `report member` (those three only count
  settling transfers from now on). The output of `transfer add` doesn't
  change.
- Each stored transfer gets a boolean `settles` key next to `note`. Transfers
  already in a store file from before this change have no such key -- treat
  those as settling, because that is what they were recorded as.
- `transfer list` marks settling transfers by appending ` [settles]` as the
  very last thing on the line (after the ` (NOTE)` part when there is one),
  e.g. `1: 2024-03-02 10.00 Ben -> Ana (pizza) [settles]`. Non-settling
  transfers print exactly as before.

Everything else is unchanged.
