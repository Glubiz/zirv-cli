The flat splits the books by calendar month, so I want a monthly view next to
the per-member report. Add `python -m ledgerlite report month YYYY-MM
--store PATH`, which looks only at expenses whose `date` falls in that
month and prints:

```
Month: 2024-03
Ana: paid 30.00 share 10.00 net 20.00
Ben: paid 0.00 share 10.00 net -10.00
Cara: paid 0.00 share 10.00 net -10.00
```

One line per member in the order they were added, `paid` = what they paid
out for expenses in that month, `share` = their shares of those expenses,
`net` = paid minus share (two decimals everywhere, minus sign when
negative). Transfers are not part of this report. Whoever is on the list
follows the archiving rules we already have: active members always appear
(even with zeros), and an archived member appears only if they actually paid
or had a share in an expense of that month.

If there are no expenses in that month at all it prints `No expenses in
YYYY-MM.` (the month as typed). A month that isn't a four-digit year, a
dash and a month from 01 to 12 is `error: invalid month: TEXT`.
