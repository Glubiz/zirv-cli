Change of plan on the rounding. Ana pointed out that with "leftover cents
start with the first participant" she gets the extra penny every single
time, and the flat has noticed. Let's make it rotate instead.

New rule for who gets the leftover cents: shares are still rounded down to
a whole cent first, but the leftover cents are now handed out starting from
a position that depends on the expense's id. For expense number N with P
participants (in participant order), start at participant number
`(N - 1) mod P` counting from 0, give out the leftover cents one at a time
going forward, and wrap around from the last participant back to the first
when you run off the end. So expense 1 behaves exactly like it did before,
expense 2 starts at the second participant, expense 3 at the third, and
when you run out of participants it wraps back to the first.

This replaces the old rule for every expense added from now on, equal
splits and `--split` ones alike. Expenses that are already recorded keep
the shares they were stored with -- nobody's past gets rewritten.
