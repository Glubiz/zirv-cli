Not every expense is split evenly -- Ben barely eats at home, and the
cinema trip was only for two of us. Add a `--split` option to `expense
add`:

`--split NAME=WEIGHT,NAME=WEIGHT,...`

- Only the listed members take part in the expense, in the order they are
  listed (that is the participant order now, not the order the members were
  added). Each share is proportional to the member's weight, e.g.
  `--split Ana=1,Ben=2` gives Ben twice Ana's share. Weights are positive
  whole numbers; the payer doesn't have to be one of the participants.
- Rounding works exactly like it already does for equal splits -- same
  rule for the leftover cents, nothing new to decide.
- Names match case-insensitively and are stored in their member spelling;
  the `weights` and `shares` objects in storage use the participant order.
- Errors: a name that isn't a member is `error: unknown member: NAME`; a
  malformed or non-positive weight, or anything else that isn't
  `NAME=WEIGHT` pairs, is `error: invalid split: TEXT` (TEXT being the
  whole `--split` value as typed); listing the same member twice is
  `error: duplicate member in split: NAME`.
- Without `--split` everything is exactly as before.
