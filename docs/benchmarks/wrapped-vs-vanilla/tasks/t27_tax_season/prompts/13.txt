I've been living with my rules for a bit and I changed my mind about how
ties are broken. "Longer pattern wins" is wrong in practice: I keep adding a
short, quick rule to override something and then it silently loses to some
older, longer one. New plan: when two or more of my rules match with the
SAME priority, the most recently added rule wins (the highest id),
regardless of how long the patterns are. Priority still comes first, and my
rules still always beat the built-ins -- only the tie-break changes.

This should hold everywhere rules get applied (`categorize`, `import`,
`merge`), not just one of them.
