Picking this back up after a handoff, so a quick recap of where the notes
feature stands: note ids are always negative (-1, -2, -3, ... in creation
order, never reused, never colliding with a transaction id), `note-add`
rejects text over 200 characters, and `note-list`/`note-add` both support
`--pinned`.
