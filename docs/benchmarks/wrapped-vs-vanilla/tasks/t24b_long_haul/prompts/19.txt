Plain `summary` (no flags) still has the currency-mixing problem too --
only `--vs-budget` got fixed. I use plain `summary` far more often, so
let's flip the default: from now on, `summary` converts every transaction
to its USD equivalent (same conversion rule as always) before totalling
and computing each category's share, by default. Keep the old,
unconverted, raw-per-currency behavior available behind a new
`--raw-currency` flag, for anyone who wants to see the numbers as-entered.

`--vs-budget` already always converts to USD and is unaffected by this
(no change needed there).
